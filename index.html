Igor Shakhanov is an independent researcher working at the intersection of philosophy of mind and the foundations of transhumanism. His central project, Causa Sui, develops a working theory of subjecthood grounded in the irreversibility of a substrate's physical state — arguing that deterministic digital architectures are structurally incapable of the kind of subjectivity that living substrates instantiate.

Recent work extends this line of inquiry into the philosophical literature on mind uploading and personal identity, examining an assumption shared by both proponents and critics of digital immortality: that a digital system is capable of being a subject in the first place.
